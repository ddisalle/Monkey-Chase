🐒 MONKEY CHASE

Choose Your Food

How to Play

  1. Choose your food.
  2. Your food starts in the bottom-left corner.
  3. Reach the basket in the top-right corner.
  4. Move up, down, left, or right. No diagonal moves.
  5. Each game has exactly 3 trees and 2 rocks.
  6. Trees and rocks block movement.
  7. The monkey moves after every move and chases you.
  8. If the monkey becomes adjacent to you, the game ends.
  9. Your score is the number of moves used to reach the basket. Lower is better!
